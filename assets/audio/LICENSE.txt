Dragon Valley audio (assets/audio)

Every sound effect and music loop in this folder is an original work, synthesized by this
repository's own scripts (scripts/audio) from the JSON recipes in assets/audio/recipes. No samples,
recordings, sample libraries or third-party audio are used, and the music quotes no existing
melody, including traditional folk tunes.

The audio is part of Dragon Valley and is licensed under the same MIT License as the rest of the
repository (Copyright (c) 2026 Ruslan Mukhamedov); see licenses.txt or the repository LICENSE.

Provenance (generator, recipe digests and the SHA-256 of every file) is recorded in the
repository at assets/audio/provenance.json. Running `npm run audio:build` regenerates identical
bytes on any platform.
